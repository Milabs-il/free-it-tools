Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short: we don’t collect, store or sell personal data. The site has no sign-up, no cookies, and no tracking or analytics.

Who we are

tools.milabs.co.il is operated by G2000 under the MiLabs brand. This document explains what data is processed when you use the site.

Tools that run only in your browser

In these tools all processing happens on your device, and nothing you enter is sent to any server:

  • Email header analyzer
  • DMARC report analyzer (reading the files themselves)
  • Certificate & CSR decoder
  • JWT decoder
  • Password generator
  • Encode & hash
  • QR generator
  • Subnet calculator
  • SPF, DMARC and Microsoft 365 record generators
  • What’s my browser

Tools that contact external services

Some tools need data from the internet. When you use them, your browser contacts the services below directly and sends them the domain name or IP address you entered. As with any internet request, these services also see your IP address and basic browser details, and they are subject to their own privacy policies.

  • Cloudflare and Google (DNS-over-HTTPS resolvers): DNS, SPF, DMARC and DKIM checks, the full domain check, the Microsoft 365 check, and host-name lookups in the DMARC report analyzer.
  • Cloudflare Security, AliDNS and AdGuard: only in the resolver comparison tool.
  • crt.sh: in the certificates & subdomains tool.
  • Microsoft (login.microsoftonline.com): in the Microsoft 365 check, to look up the tenant ID.

These requests don’t pass through us, and we have no access to them.

Tools that go through our server

Three tools need a server: "What’s my IP", the SSL check and the blacklist check. When you use them, your browser sends our server (on Zoho Catalyst) the domain or IP address you entered, and the server also sees your IP address. In "What’s my IP" the address is shown back to you.

Our server connects to the server you asked to check (SSL check), or sends DNS queries to the SpamCop, UCEPROTECT, PSBL, Mailspike, DroneBL, NiX Spam and s5h.net blocklists. Those services see our server’s address, not yours.

We don’t store the requests or the results. To prevent abuse, the server counts in memory how many requests each IP address made in the last minute, and the count is discarded after a minute.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Zoho Catalyst. As with any website, the infrastructure providers (hosting and DNS) may log technical data about requests to the site, such as IP address, date and time, and the page loaded, for operations and security. We don’t use this data to identify visitors.

The site’s fonts are hosted on the site itself, so there are no requests to Google Fonts or any other font service.

Contacting us

If you email us, we’ll use the details you provide only to handle your request.

Your rights

Under privacy law you may ask to access, correct or delete data about you. Because we don’t collect data on the site, there usually won’t be any, but you can always contact us at privacy@milabs.co.il.

Open source

The site’s code is open, so anyone can check for themselves what is sent and where: github.com/Milabs-il/free-it-tools.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that process more data, we’ll update this policy beforehand and show the date of the change at the top of the page.
