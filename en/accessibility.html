Accessibility statement

Last updated: 16 November 2026

We want everyone to be able to use these tools, including people with disabilities. The site was built to the Israeli standard IS 5568, which is based on WCAG 2.0 level AA, and was tested against WCAG 2.1 level AA.

What we’ve done

  • Every tool can be used with a keyboard alone, with a clear indicator of the focused element.
  • A "Skip to content" link at the top of every page.
  • A consistent heading and landmark structure that makes screen-reader navigation easier.
  • Every form field has a label.
  • Text color contrast of at least 4.5:1.
  • Result status (OK, warning, error) is shown with an icon and text, not color alone, and is read out by screen readers.
  • New results are announced to screen readers when they appear.
  • The site works on small screens and phones.
  • The site respects the system’s reduced-motion setting.
  • The site is available in English and Hebrew.

How the site was tested

All 20 tools, in both languages, were checked automatically with axe-core against WCAG 2.1 levels A and AA, including states where results are shown, and passed. Keyboard navigation and the phone layout were also checked in Chrome.

Known limitations

  • A full manual test with screen readers such as NVDA and VoiceOver hasn’t been done yet. If you run into a problem, please tell us.
  • A QR code is an image. Its content is also available as text in the field it was created from.
  • Technical values such as DNS records and certificates are shown as-is, in Latin characters.
  • Links to external sites such as crt.sh and GitHub lead to sites we don’t control.

Found a problem?

If something on the site isn’t accessible to you, write to us with the page or tool and what went wrong. We’ll do our best to reply and fix it quickly.
