Free IT tools: domain, email and security

Free tools for domains, DNS and email, Microsoft 365, security and networking. No sign-up, and most of them run entirely in your browser.

Full domain check

One check that runs everything that matters: DNS, MX, SPF, DMARC, DKIM, DNSSEC, CAA, MTA-STS and more.

DNS Lookup

Look up DNS records of any type. Enter an IP address to get its PTR (reverse DNS) record.

SPF check

Analyzes the SPF record: follows every include, counts DNS lookups (the limit is 10) and catches common mistakes.

DMARC check

Breaks down the DMARC record, explains each tag and recommends how to improve spoofing protection.

DKIM check

Checks a DKIM key by selector. Leave the selector empty to scan common selectors (Microsoft 365, Google, Zoho and more).

Microsoft 365 check

Checks whether the domain is set up correctly for Microsoft 365: tenant ID, MX, Autodiscover, SPF, DKIM, Intune and Teams.

Resolver comparison

Runs the same query against several public DNS resolvers. Useful to see whether a DNS change has propagated, and how much TTL is left.

Email header analyzer

Paste a message’s full headers (Message headers / Show original). The analysis runs only in your browser – nothing is sent to any server.

SSL certificates & subdomains

Searches every SSL certificate issued for the domain (Certificate Transparency). Reveals subdomains, including forgotten ones, and certificates that are about to expire.

Data comes from crt.sh. For large domains the search can take up to 30 seconds.

SPF generator

Pick the services that send email for your domain and get an SPF record ready to copy.

DMARC generator

Build a valid DMARC record, with an explanation of every option.

DMARC report analyzer

Drop DMARC reports you received by email (.xml, .gz or .zip files) and get a clear picture: who sends mail as your domain, and what passes or fails.

Microsoft 365 DNS record generator

Every record you need to add at your DNS provider to connect a domain to Microsoft 365.

Certificate & CSR decoder

Paste a certificate or a certificate signing request (CSR) in PEM format. Decoding happens only in your browser.

Password generator

Strong random passwords, created in your browser with a cryptographic generator. Nothing is sent or stored.

JWT decoder

Paste a token (for example a Microsoft Entra access token) to see its contents, when it expires and what each claim means. Decoding is local only.

Subnet calculator

Calculates network range, broadcast, address count and mask. Supports IPv4 and IPv6.

Encode & hash

Base64, URL, Hex and hashes (MD5, SHA). Everything runs in your browser.

What’s my browser

Your browser, operating system and screen details. Handy when tech support asks “Which browser are you using?”

Decode another User-Agent

What’s my IP

The public IP address you’re browsing from, as our server sees it.

SSL certificate check

Checks the SSL certificate of a website or server: validity, certificate chain, hostname match and supported TLS versions. Works for VPN portals too.

The check runs from our server. Only common TLS service ports are supported.

Blacklist check

Checks whether a mail server’s IP address appears on blocklists (DNSBLs). Enter an IP address, or a domain to check its MX servers.

DNS queries go straight from your browser to public DNS-over-HTTPS resolvers (Cloudflare and Google), and certificate searches go to crt.sh. The SSL check, blacklist check and "What’s my IP" go through our server, with nothing stored. Everything else – header and report analysis, certificate and token decoding, passwords and encoding – runs only in your browser and is never sent anywhere. The site uses no cookies or trackers.
